Targets are what the scorer is trained on: no collision = no overlap with any vehicle within 5 s (plan continued 1 s at its final velocity), on road = no footprint corner off the drivable map within 4 s, TTC = no vehicle ahead overlapped by the box carried 0.3/0.6/0.9 s at its current velocity (to 3 s), in lane = max distance from the lane centre ≤ 1.75 m, progress = min(1, travel ÷ human travel over 4 s), final = NC·DAC·(5·EP + 5·TTC + 2·LK)/12. Predictions are the average of 5 probe+geometry scorers from the fold where this scene was held out. Formula = σ(NC)⁴·σ(DAC)·(5·EP + 5·TTC + 2·LK)/12. The gate overrides the released pick only if its predicted no-collision is below 0.5.

Flags: R = released pick, P = trained scorer's pick, B = best plan by the training target. Keys ← → step ticks. The URL remembers the episode.
