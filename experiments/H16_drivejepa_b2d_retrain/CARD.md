# H16 · Drive-JEPA's training rebuilt on Bench2Drive: encoder LoRA, own vocabulary, own grade, Stage 2

**Navvrat** · Bench2Drive, KITScenes, nuScenes, neuroNCAP, NAVSIM, AlpaSim, offline · ⏳ running · 5 Nov 2026 · hypothesis

[← all experiments](../../README.md#hypothesis-experiments) · [the account](README.md) · [experiment.toml](experiment.toml)

**Builds on:** [H07](../H07_encoder_lora/CARD.md), [GC04](../GC04_bench2drive/CARD.md)

## Question

Released Drive-JEPA was trained on NAVSIM and drives paddock's 50 Bench2Drive routes to a mean DS of 57.60 at 2 Hz; GC04's Final reached 73.6 by retraining parts of it, with the encoder frozen and same-spot twins of eval routes in its training set. If everything Drive-JEPA's training rests on is rebuilt from Bench2Drive instead (the encoder adapted by hide-and-guess, a vocabulary from the expert's paths, a grade from Bench2Drive's own penalties, Stage 2 on those), with the eval routes and their trigger twins kept out, what does the model do, on the routes and on the regression gate?

## Hypothesis

Rebuilding Drive-JEPA's training on Bench2Drive's own data and scoring terms gives a model that drives the 50 routes better than the released model, whose DS there is 57.60 at 2 Hz. No effect size or refutation rule was written before the runs: this wording is read from the specs, not taken from them.

## Predictions

⚠️ Not pre-registered: the specs of 5 Nov fix the recipe and what to report, not an expected effect, and nothing was registered here before the runs, though the rule already stood

## What we learned

So far, and none of it a Bench2Drive driving score. Offline on the 47 held-out clips, epoch 18's pick grades .894 on the 4 s grade, against .983 for the expert and .981 for the best of its 32 (closest ADE .240 m, pick ADE 2.23 m). On the 13 of 50 routes that finished before the run was stopped, its mean DS is 31.73, against 42.16 for the released model and 45.36 for Run 3A on the same routes; those 13 are the front of the queue, not a sample of the 50. The regression gate, run by hand, says FAIL: KITScenes 2.537 -> 3.159, nuScenes 2.431 -> 3.796 and neuroNCAP 3.679 -> 1.820 regress, with the candidate on crop native and FOLLOW sent as straight; NAVSIM, AlpaSim and the gate's Bench2Drive subset have no result yet.
