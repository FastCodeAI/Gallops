## Read with these numbers

- **Paths against sizes.** 36,897 training paths, 33,778 of them distinct (16 numbers, rounded to 1e-6 m). That is 4.5 paths per group on average at 8,192.
- **The training rows of coverage are in-sample.** Training paths that are themselves a vocabulary path (ADE under 1e-5 m): 256: 0, 512: 4, 1,024: 18, 2,048: 125, 4,096: 893, 8,192: 4,071. The held-out paths were not used to build any vocabulary.
- **Stationary paths.** Training: 3,006 exactly zero, 6,501 (17.6%) under 0.1 m long, 9,871 (26.8%) under 1 m. Held-out: 258 exactly zero, 596 (26.5%) under 0.1 m, 780 (34.7%) under 1 m. So the train and held-out rows average over different shares of stationary paths. All paths are weighted the same. The largest group at every size is the stationary one (groups table).
- **Held-out max.** The held-out max at 8,192 is `InterurbanAdvancedActorFlow_Town12_Route1030_Weather8` frame 75, ADE 2.113 m. Its nearest path at 2,048 and 4,096 has the same 8 points (largest gap 0.0e+00 m), so the three rows show the same value.
- **NAVSIM's largest distance** on the training paths is `SignalizedJunctionRightTurn_Town13_Route583_Weather11` frame 95, ADE 8.07 m (expert ends at x 7.4, y -34.7 m, heading -96.9 degrees).
- **Groups and coverage use different distances.** k-means groups by squared distance over the 16 numbers; coverage uses ADE. A path's nearest vocabulary path need not be its group's centre.
